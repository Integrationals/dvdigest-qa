Your standards

Model yours on ours

Data Vault practitioners make different choices about business keys, how satellites are split, where integration happens, how releases are tested and promoted. This vault is one way; where yours differs, we want to follow yours.

Each release includes its DDL, infra/snowflake/ddl/vault.sql and mart.sql: bring it into ER/Studio or a similar modeling tool, model your standard, and send it back, by email or on a branch of your own. We build it into the vault and send you the pull request, so you review your approach in the actual code.
