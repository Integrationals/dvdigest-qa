An acquired agency's business: what's in it, and what you can prove about it.

DV Digest integrates an acquired agency's business, invented for this exercise, in a classic Data Vault on Snowflake, built with dbt and datavault4dbt, which builds the same vault on Databricks. It answers two kinds of question: what is in the agency's business, and what you can show a regulator about it.

Every record keeps its source and its full history. A record that arrives in a form the vault doesn't accept is reported as a finding, not dropped. Every answer names the query behind it. On that base we propose eight compliance questions for a distributor, among them:

  • which sales were made by an agent not licensed or appointed for that line, state and date;
  • which Medicare appointments lacked a documented scope of appointment;
  • which seniors' policies were replaced soon after purchase without the replacement disclosure.

When the vault flags a case like these, it will name the regulation the case breaks.

v2026.10.1, The Agency's Business, answers four questions about the agency's business. v2026.11.1, The Site and the Data Model, adds where each record came from, when it was first known and what it said on any date. Next, v2026.11.2 brings the business vault and the compliance questions.

Every change is built in development, passes six checks in QA, then is built in production and compared with QA row for row. You can open the vault's full model, keys and relationships included, in ER/Studio or a similar modeling tool.
