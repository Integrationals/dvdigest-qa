Course DIGEST Data Vault Course

1. What the workbook is

The textbook teaches; the workbook is where you practice. After each lesson from lesson 2 on, the textbook sends you here for its lab: a small increment of the same vault, built in your own branch and your own environment, which merges into qa beside the teacher’s work. Your increments add what the teacher’s do not have. From time to time the workbook also carries a defect drill: a symptom reported from qa, for you to trace, test and fix.

3. Where landing data comes from

The vault starts at landing; filling landing is outside it. In this course the teacher stands in for the sources: the teacher creates each new landing table and delivers its rows to LANDING, the production landing database, and your landing database receives them only by being cloned from LANDING again. So a lab never writes to landing: it starts by declaring a landing table as a source and builds the vault from there. When a lab needs data your clone does not yet hold, the teacher refreshes it first.

4. How a lab runs

  1. Make a short lab branch from rayserrano/dig-574-labs, named for the lab.
  2. Read the lab’s sheet: its lesson, the question it moves forward, the starting state and the tasks. The shared delivery documents (profile, design, test plan, implementation guide) hold the full detail; the sheet points to the steps you take.
  3. Before writing code, run the lab’s checks and see which fail; then make them pass. Hints sit behind toggles: try first.
  4. Run the full build in your environment; every test must pass.
  5. Open one pull request from the lab branch into rayserrano/dig-574-labs. The teacher reviews it the way lesson 8 describes.

1. The goal

Pinecrest Mutual is a second carrier, invented for the course. Its policy data now sits in your landing database as PINECREST.POLICY: twelve rows over two nights, eight on October 1 and four on October 2, every column text, as Pinecrest sends it. Your lab makes that table a source of the vault: declared to dbt, with the two tests every landed source carries. From here, Labs 1-3 to 1.7 carry its rows into the vault one layer at a time.

4. What to notice

Lesson 2 said every row the vault keeps carries where it came from and when. Look at what PINECREST.POLICY already gives you for that: LOADED_AT, as text. The record source, PMC, does not exist in the table: Lab 1-3 adds it, in staging. Which of the two does the source itself carry, and which does the vault add?

Check yourself

The when arrives with the data: LOADED_AT, as text, which staging will turn into the load timestamp. The where does not: the record source code, PMC, is added by the vault in staging (Lab 1-3), and named in the source system seed (Lab 1-7).
