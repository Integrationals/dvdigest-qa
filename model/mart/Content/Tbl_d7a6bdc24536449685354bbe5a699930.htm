Definition

CLIENT_POLICY_ROLE_FACT Columns 
ColumnName  Domain  Datatype  NULL  Definition 
CLIENT_POLICY_ROLE_KEY    VARCHAR(16777216)  NO  
The row''s key, the vault''s key for one client, one policy and one role.
 
CLIENT_KEY    VARCHAR(16777216)  YES  
The client, a client_dim key; the unknown client''s key when the sale gives no client number.
 
POLICY_KEY    VARCHAR(16777216)  YES  
The policy, a policy_dim key; the unknown policy''s key for a sale with a blank policy number.
 
ROLE    VARCHAR(16)  YES  
What the client is on the policy, as the sales data sends it, owner, insured or payor.
 
SOLD_DATE    DATE  YES  
The day the sale was made, from the current version of the sale; empty when the sale gives none.
 
FIRST_KNOWN_DATE    DATE  YES  
The day the vault first received this role on this policy.
