Definition  
Hub of Sunbelt Senior Insurance client numbers (increment 1 design D-3): one row per client key, with the load timestamp and record source of the key''s first arrival, plus the two ghost records.\n

CLIENT_NUMBER    VARCHAR(32)  YES  
Sunbelt Senior''s client number as first sent; its trimmed, upper-case form is hashed into the key.

The load time: when the vault received this row, in UTC.
