v2026.11.1 The Site and the Data Model

In QACertified2 new, 2 refined
As of the QA build of 2026-11-01, 16:41 UTC (run 70403231595822, commit 4ad7057). Every number below was asked through DV Digest and checked against a direct query; replaced by the production build at promotion.

What we knew, and when: where each client's record came from and when it was first known, and what a carrier stated about each policy on each date. Two answers from release 1 are refined.

1. New questions you can ask

Q-13. Where did each client's record come from, and when did we first know it? Every client record came from Sunbelt Senior's client file. Clients first known on 2026-10-01 are on 8 policies, in 11 client roles, and the client first known on 2026-10-02 on 1 policy, in 1 role. One sale named no client; it is counted against an unknown client, which no source sent.

Q-14. What did we know about each policy on a given date, and what changed since? Northfield Life sent 10 versions of terms for 9 policies. One policy changed: NF-100003 was in force on 2026-10-01 and lapsed on 2026-10-02, its face amount of 15,000 dollars unchanged.

2. Changed answers

Q-11. Which agent sold each policy, and where do our sources disagree about it? The sources agree about the agent who sold it on 14 policies and disagree on 1. Release 1 said 15: one sale sent policy NF-100005 without its carrier, and that policy was counted twice.

Q-15. How many records arrive in a form we don't accept, from which source? Still 3, all from Sunbelt Senior's client file; beside them, 6 rows arrived without a client, policy, insured or agent number: 5 from Sunbelt Senior and 1 from Northfield Life.

Q-10. How much coverage does each carrier provide through us? Northfield Life: 110,000 dollars, from its in-force policies. Release 1 said 125,000: policy NF-100003 lapsed on 2026-10-02, and a lapsed policy provides no coverage.

3. Terms

  • First known: the day the vault first received a record, the moment it entered what we know.
  • Version of a policy's terms: what a carrier stated about a policy, its status and face amount, from one delivery until the next that changed it.
  • Coverage: the face amount a carrier states for a policy, in dollars, from its current terms.
  • Record not accepted: a record that arrived in a form the vault does not accept, such as a client number written another way.
  • Row missing a number: a row that arrived without a client, policy, insured or agent number; kept and flagged, and counted once however many numbers it lacks.

4. What to know

  • Q-14 shows what a carrier stated, version by version. Only Northfield Life sends terms today.
  • One sale named no client, and Q-13 counts it against an unknown client rather than leaving it out.
  • One sale sent policy NF-100005 without its carrier. The vault keeps the two records apart until a later release joins them; counted by number, it counts once.
  • Pinecrest Mutual, a second carrier, has sent its own name so far, and no policies.
  • Release 1's page suggested prompts written in the measures' internal names. Those names are retired; ask in your own words.
  • The data is invented for the DIGEST Data Vault course: two deliveries from Sunbelt Senior and Northfield Life, on 2026-10-01 and 2026-10-02.
