Plain words

Terms

Clienta client number Sunbelt Senior, the agency the distributor acquired, sends for a person it serves.
Policyan insurance contract issued by a carrier, identified by the carrier and its policy number together.
Carrierthe insurance company that issues a policy. In this release, Northfield Life.
Rolewhat a client is on a policy: owner, insured or payor.
Agenta licensed producer, identified by a national producer number.
Face amountthe death benefit a carrier states for a policy, in dollars.
Rejected recorda record that arrived in a form the vault does not accept, such as a client number written another way.

Before you read the numbers

What to know

Face amounts appear only where a carrier sends them. In this release only Northfield Life does.
One sale arrived without a carrier code (policy NF-100005). It counts as a policy, with no carrier and no amount.
One sale arrived without a policy number. Its client's role counts against an unknown policy, shown as “(unknown)”.
“Sources disagree” means the agency's sales data and the carrier's policy data name different agents as the one who sold the same policy.
The data is invented for the DIGEST Data Vault course: two deliveries from Sunbelt Senior and Northfield Life, on 2026-10-01 and 2026-10-02.
