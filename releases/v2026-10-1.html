v2026.10.1 The Agency's Business

What the distributor bought with Sunbelt Senior: the policies its clients are on, with which carriers and in which roles; how much coverage each carrier provides; which agent sold each policy, and where the sources disagree; and how much of what arrived could not be accepted.

1. What you can ask

Q-9. For each client, which policies are they on, with which carrier, and in what role? Sunbelt Senior's clients are the owner on 9 policies, the insured on 3 and the payor on 1. A client can hold more than one role on one policy.

Q-10. How much coverage does each carrier provide through us? Northfield Life: 125,000.00 dollars in face amount.

Q-11. Which agent sold each policy, and where do our sources disagree about it? Sunbelt Senior's sales data and Northfield Life's policy data agree about the agent who sold it on 15 policies and disagree on 1.

Q-15. How many records arrive in a form we don't accept, from which source? 3, all from Sunbelt Senior.

2. Try these

Ask your Claude with the DV Digest connector. Expected results are as of the date above.

  1. “With DV Digest, show policies_held by client_policy__role.” Expected: owner 9, insured 3, payor 1.
  2. “With DV Digest, show face_amount_total by policy__carrier_code.” Expected: NFL 125000.00, and one row with no carrier and no amount.
  3. “With DV Digest, show policies_written by agent_policy__sources_disagree.” Expected: false 15, true 1.
  4. “With DV Digest, show findings by finding__source_system.” Expected: SUNBELT 3.
  5. “With DV Digest, list the metrics and their dimensions.” Expected: seven metrics.

3. Terms

  • Client: a client number Sunbelt Senior, the agency the distributor acquired, sends for a person it serves.
  • Policy: an insurance contract issued by a carrier, identified by the carrier and its policy number together.
  • Carrier: the insurance company that issues a policy. In this release, Northfield Life.
  • Role: what a client is on a policy: owner, insured or payor.
  • Agent: a licensed producer, identified by a national producer number.
  • Face amount: the death benefit a carrier states for a policy, in dollars.
  • Rejected record: a record that arrived in a form the vault does not accept, such as a client number written another way.

4. What to know

  • v2026.11.1 corrected coverage to count in-force policies only: a lapsed policy provides no coverage, so Northfield Life's coverage became 110,000 dollars, NF-100003 having lapsed on 2026-10-02. The figures on this page are as this release was answered.
  • Face amounts appear only where a carrier sends them. In this release only Northfield Life does.
  • One sale arrived without a carrier code (policy NF-100005). It counts as a policy, with no carrier and no amount.
  • One sale arrived without a policy number. Its client's role counts against an unknown policy, shown as “(unknown)”.
  • “Sources disagree” means the agency's sales data and the carrier's policy data name different agents as the one who sold the same policy.
  • The data is invented for the DIGEST Data Vault course: two deliveries from Sunbelt Senior and Northfield Life, on 2026-10-01 and 2026-10-02.
